Every now and then, it is quite convinient to get notifications right to your smartphone from your IoT device, e.g. when somebody rings your doorbell, a measurements reaches a threshold etc. My messaging service of choice is [Matrix](https://www.matrix.io) because it is decentralized and open. In this post I will guide you through the steps, that are necessary to setup your Zephyr device to send you a simple text message via the chat service. Let's get started!

# What you need

There is only one thing you need: a simple hardware that can connect to the internet to send HTTP requests, e.g., via WiFi or cellular IoT (LTE-M / NB-IoT).
